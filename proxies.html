How to Use

  • Enter card names or IDs, one per line
  • Use quantity notation: "4x Ariel - On Human Legs" for multiple copies
  • Card IDs should be in format "001-001" or card names like "Ariel - On Human Legs"
  • Partial names work too: "ariel on legs" will find "Ariel - On Human Legs"
  • Click "Load Cards" to preview your selection
  • Click "Generate PDF" to create a printable sheet with 8 cards
  • Print the PDF and cut out the cards for proxies
