How to Use

  1. Enter your card list in the text area below, one card per line
  2. Click "Load Cards" to find and preview your cards
  3. Review the preview to ensure all cards were found correctly
  4. Click "Generate PDF" to create your printable proxy sheet
  5. Print the PDF at 100% scale (no scaling/fitting)
  6. Cut out the cards along the borders for your proxies

Notes

  • Quantity notation: Use "4x Ariel - On Human Legs" for multiple copies of the same card
  • Card formats: Accepts card IDs ("001-001"), full names ("Ariel - On Human Legs"), or partial names ("ariel legs")
  • Smart matching: The system will find close matches if your spelling isn't exact
  • PDF layout: Each page fits 8 cards (4×2 grid) in landscape orientation
  • Print settings: Use 100% scale when printing - do not "fit to page" or the cards will be the wrong size
  • Card size: Cards are sized to approximate standard trading card dimensions
  • Images: Card images are fetched from Dreamborn.ink - some cards may show placeholders if images aren't available
