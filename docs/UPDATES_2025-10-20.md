## EV Calculation Improvements

### Pack Model (config/pack_model.json)
```
Cards per pack: 12
Packs per box: 24
Boxes per case: 4

Slots:
- 2 rare-or-higher slots
- 1 foil slot
- 6 common slots
- 3 uncommon slots

Rarity Odds (rare slot):
- Rare: 67.65%
- Super Rare: 19.77%
- Legendary: 8.33%
- Epic: 4.17%
- Iconic: 0.08%
```

### EV Methodology
1. Categorize cards by rarity
2. Calculate trimmed mean (removes outliers)
3. Apply rarity odds from pack model
4. Sum contributions from all slots
5. Scale to box/case

## Known Issues

### Promo Card Pricing Discrepancies
**Issue**: Large differences between Dreamborn and JustTCG for promo foils
**Cards affected**: P1-, D23- prefix cards
**Root cause**: Different market sources or timing
**Resolution**: Manual verification recommended for high-value promos

### Limited Box Pricing
**Issue**: Only Set 9 (Fabled) has box/case pricing
**Root cause**: JustTCG only tracks current sealed products
**Resolution**: This is expected behavior

### cards-formatted.json Staleness
**Issue**: Was last updated in September
**Root cause**: Not automatically fetched
**Resolution**: ✓ Fixed - now auto-updated from Dreamborn
